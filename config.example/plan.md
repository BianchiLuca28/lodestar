# Financial plan: Marta Esempio (fake)

> Fake persona shipped with Lodestar. Every name, account and number here is invented.
> Rules in `rules.yaml` point to the numbered sections below.

## 1 Profile

Marta, 29, employee in Bologna, net salary €2,150 a month (13 payments). Rents a flat and wants to buy a home around mid-2031. No debts.

## 2 Goals

1. Keep day-to-day liquidity calm and predictable.
2. Hold an emergency fund she never touches.
3. Save the house down payment in safe, dated instruments.
4. Grow long-term wealth with a single global ETF, untouched for decades.

## 4 Pillars

### 4.1 P1 Liquidity

Banca Aurora current account, plus cash and the prepaid card. Pays every expense.

### 4.2 P2 Emergency fund

XEON (money-market ETF) on Broker Orsa, plus the postal savings book. Only for real emergencies.

### 4.3 P3 House

Italian and German government bonds on Broker Orsa, all maturing on or before the expected house date (June 2031).

### 4.4 P4 Long term

VWCE on Broker Orsa via the monthly savings plan. Horizon: 25+ years.

## 5 Rules

### 5.1 Never touch

Money never leaves P2 or P4, except a real emergency (P2) or retirement (P4).

### 5.2 Liquidity range

Banca Aurora stays between €1,200 and €2,800.

- **Safety valve:** if it drops below €1,200, next month's transfer is halved (€175 to P3, €150 to P4).
- **Surplus:** at the end of each quarter, anything above €2,800 is cut back to €2,400; the excess goes to P3 until the end of 2029, then to P4.

### 5.3 Emergency fund

P2 holds at least 6 months of average spending (12-month average).

### 5.4 House bonds

1. Every P3 bond matures on or before the expected house date.
2. Less than 12 months before that date, new P3 money goes to XEON instead.
3. One order a quarter (end of January, April, July, October) with all P3 cash on Broker Orsa, alternating BTP and Bund on the furthest pair (BTP-01GN31, Bund-15FB31). BTPs in whole lots of €1,000 nominal; the remainder waits for the next quarter.
4. Compare bonds on gross yield.
5. A bond that matures is reinvested within 30 days in the same country's pair.

### 5.5 Long term

When P4 passes €8,000, review whether to add an emerging-markets ETF. Never automatic.

### 5.6 Spending

Discretionary spending (eating out, fun, shopping, travel, subscriptions) stays at or below €550 a month, net of refunds in the same category.
